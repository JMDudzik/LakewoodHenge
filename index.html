Lakewood, Ohio

Lakewoodhenge

When the setting sun lines up with the streets of Lakewood.

What is it?

A few evenings a year, the sun sets in line with a city's street grid and pours straight down the avenues, much as it aligns with the stones at Stonehenge on the solstice.

Lakewood has a grid, a western horizon, and Lake Erie sunsets worth stopping for. This is our version.

Next alignment

Detroit Avenue sunrise · March 7–10, 2027

Look east from Bunts Road, a little before 7:00 AM.

Two avenues, two angles

Madison Avenue runs almost perfectly straight across the city, pointing just 1° north of due west. That puts its alignment right at the equinox, when the sun rises at one end of the street and sets at the other in the same week.

Detroit Avenue is two straights joined by a curve. East of Bunts Road it tilts 7° north of west, so the sun finds it about two weeks away from the equinox. West of Warren Road it runs close to Madison's line.

The 2027 calendar

Each window opens the day the sun sits on the horizon in line with the street and closes the day it lines up a little higher, clear of rooftops and trees. A day either side is still close.

Where to stand

Sunset

Madison, near W 117th

Anywhere between W 117th and Bunts, looking west. Nearly two miles of straight street run out ahead of you.

Sunrise

Madison, near Warren

Around Warren Road or Elmwood Avenue, looking east. The same long straight, in reverse.

Sunset

Detroit, near W 117th

Looking west toward Bunts along Detroit's straightest mile.

Sunrise

Detroit, near Bunts

Looking east toward W 117th as the sun comes up over the city.

Watch from the sidewalk or a corner, never from the roadway. These spots and dates are worked out from street maps and the sun's position; trees, buildings and clouds will have their say, so come a day early and scout.
